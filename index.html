📖 The Magical Forest

Once Upon a Time... ✨

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl named Lily in a beautiful village. She loved nature, flowers, birds and animals. One sunny morning, Lily went for a walk in the forest. Suddenly, she saw a beautiful golden butterfly flying near her. The butterfly slowly guided her into a mysterious forest. There she discovered a beautiful golden door. When Lily opened the door, she entered a magical world filled with talking trees, colorful birds and beautiful fairies.

The Little Rabbit 🐰

Suddenly, Lily saw a little rabbit crying. The rabbit had lost all its friends. Lily decided to help the rabbit. She searched everywhere in the magical forest and finally found all its friends. All the animals were very happy. The forest fairy thanked Lily and gave her a magical star as a gift. Lily returned home happily, remembering her wonderful adventure forever.

❤️ Moral: Helping others is the greatest magic in the world!
✨ The End ✨
